Pasta Aglio e Olio

One of the first recpies I've ever leaned using just 7 ingredients!

And I remember how pround I was, here we are going to make it using pre-made past. But if you ever have the time, making the pasta strands yourself is really worth it!

Ingredients

  • Pasta
  • Olive oil
  • Garlic
  • Parsly
  • Lemon
  • Salt
  • Pepper

Follow in order

  1. First cook the pasts in boiling water, don't forget to add salt to the water. It should take 10 to 15 minutes for store-bought pasta strands.
  2. At the same time suate the garlics that have been thinly sliced.
  3. When the pasta is ready add it to the garlics, add some of the pasta water.
  4. Add all of the remaing ingreding with salt and pepper. And toss, toss it a lot, it helps the sauce to emosify
  5. Serve only to your loved ones, because it is not made easliy!
